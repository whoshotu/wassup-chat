Wassup Decoder PRO

Thank you for purchasing Wassup Decoder Pro! This premium Chrome Extension brings real-time translation directly into your streaming chats, private messages, and web browsing experience.

🚀 How to Install

Because this is a private premium extension, you will install it manually in Developer Mode. Don't worry, it takes less than 30 seconds!

Step 1: Extract the .zip file you just downloaded into a normal folder on your computer. Keep this folder somewhere safe (like your Documents).
Step 2: Open Google Chrome and type chrome://extensions/ into your web address bar and press Enter.
Step 3: Look in the top right corner of the Extensions page and turn ON the switch that says "Developer mode".
Step 4: Click the "Load unpacked" button that appeared in the top left, and select the unzipped folder from Step 1! The extension is now installed.

🔐 How to Activate

1. Click the "Puzzle Piece" icon in the top right of your Chrome browser and "Pin" the Wassup Decoder extension so you can easily see it.
2. Click the extension icon to open the dashboard.
3. Enter your Ko-fi Order ID to activate your 30-day premium license.
4. Select your Native Language from the dropdown, and you are ready to go!

✨ Features & How to Use

1. The Auto-Injector (Streaming Chat)

Simply open your Chaturbate or Stripchat streaming page. Keep the extension running, and any foreign language chat messages will magically have English (or your native language) injected directly next to them in a yellow highlight pill.

2. Global Webpage Translator (Discord, Twitter, etc)

Open the extension dashboard and check the box for "Auto-Translate Webpages (Global Mode)". When this is on, the extension will automatically scan and translate every piece of text on whatever website you are looking at seamlessly.

3. Universal Highlight Translator

If you don't want the Global Translator turned on, you can manually translate anything: simply highlight a paragraph of text on any website, Right-Click it, and select "Translate with Wassup". A beautiful translation widget will pop up in the corner of your screen!

4. Custom Slang Dictionary (Overrides)

Open the dashboard to access the Custom Dictionary. Here you can train the AI by adding your own custom words! If a user says a specific slang word that the translator struggles with, add the Slang and exactly what you want it to translate to. The extension will intercept those words instantly.

🛠️ Troubleshooting & FAQ

"My extension stopped translating suddenly!"

Open the extension dashboard. Your 30-day license may have expired. If it says expired, simply purchase a renewal on Ko-fi and enter your new Order ID.

"Certain websites look broken or glitchy."

Aggressive text translation can sometimes break highly complex websites. If a website menu breaks, simply open the Extension dashboard, uncheck "Auto-Translate Webpages (Global Mode)", and refresh the page.

"I changed my translation language but the chat didn't change."

Language settings only apply to new messages that arrive after you changed the setting. Old messages are not rescanned to preserve performance.

"The right-click translate widget says 'Loading...' forever."

Simply refresh the web page you are on. Chrome sometimes disconnects background extensions to save memory if you've been on the same page for hours.
